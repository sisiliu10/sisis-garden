How it works

Size = progress. Set each project's progress with the slider. Seed → Sprout → Sapling → Budding → In bloom, and past 65% the plant keeps getting bigger until it's giant at 100%.

Skill beds sit below the hedge. A bed fills up with flowers as the skill grows. Water it whenever you practise.

Water = work. Water a plant whenever you worked on that project. Every watering asks for one line on what you did, so each plant keeps its own log (you'll find it in the plant card and on the computer in the cottage). Plants dry out over a week. Ignore one for 5+ days and weeds sprout; after 9 days the flies arrive. Watering pulls the weeds.

Walk with WASD or the arrow keys, or tap the grass. Double-tap to run. The pool is next to the fountain: walk in, or tap the diving board for a big jump. A slide stands top right: tap it, and you climb the ladder and slide down. Below the hedge, skills grow on the left and your to-dos hang on the clotheslines on the right: every piece of laundry is one to-do. Tap the basket or the line to hang up a new one, tap a piece to see it and take it down when it is done. An alpaca grazes near the fountain: tap it to ride (faster than walking, double-tap to gallop), tap yourself to get off. To move a plant or bed, open it and tap Move, then tap the new spot. Tap a plant to walk to it, press E to water.

Atmosphere. The garden follows your real clock, from sunrise to a starry night. When every plant is well watered, rain showers pass through, followed by a rainbow. Log something every day for 7 days in a row and a cat moves in.

The cottage top left: walk through the door to go inside. The computer on the desk opens a dashboard of everything you're growing, and the bookshelf keeps your reading list: reading now, want to read, and read, each with its English title and a two-line summary.

Quiz show. The arcade machine in the cottage opens a quiz show. Everyone in the same garden can join: 8 questions, 15 seconds each, faster answers score more. Pick a level (easy, medium, hard), add a topic if you like, and Claude writes the questions.

Friends. Everyone signed in sees everyone else in the same garden live: walking, swimming and chat bubbles, with names above the avatars. The dot in the chat bar shows whether you are live and how many people are here. Tap another avatar to hug, kiss, push or throw a water balloon.

Neighbours. Friends with their own garden page go under Gardens → Neighbours. Under Edit you decide per plant whether visitors see its next step and work log; the bookshelf has its own switch.

Gardens lists everyone who planted here. Anyone the page is shared with can visit; to plant their own garden they need edit access.
